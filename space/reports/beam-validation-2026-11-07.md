# Afterburner vs Pythia8 beam effects — 2026-11-07

Rebuilt from afterburner's 2021 `python/comparison.ipynb` (D-user-15, D-default-21). Code and how to run it:
`beam-validation/README.md`. Figures are in `space/plots/beam-validation/` (`10x100_comparison.png`,
`9x130_comparison.png`, `9x130-hiacc_comparison.png`), and all numbers are in `summary.csv` beside them.
Everything runs in eicweb/eic_xl:26.11.0-stable (abconv 0.2.1, Pythia8 8.316, eicSimuBeamEffects 1.1) with
50 000 events per sample and Pythia8 seed 1001. Pythia8 runs NC DIS with 10 < Q² < 100 GeV² and a −25 mrad
crossing angle.

## Result

1. **With matched optics, abconv reproduces Pythia8's eicBeamShape.**
   - The beam-angle widths of both beams, in both planes, agree within 0.6 % at 10x100 (high divergence) and
     within 1 % at 9x130 (high acceptance).
   - The vertex x, z and t widths agree within 1 %.
   - All means agree within 0.012 σ.
   - Final-state η and φ agree within 0.1 %.
   - χ²/ndf ≈ 1 everywhere except vertex y.
2. **Vertex y differs by the steering-file rounding.** Pythia8 takes `Beams:sigmaVertexY` (0.008 mm in
   high divergence, 0.011 mm in high acceptance) from the steering file. abconv computes the overlap of the two
   beams from the emittances and β*: 0.0088 and 0.0108 mm. The result is +9 % (10x100) and −3 % (9x130), at
   8–11 µm, which is irrelevant for physics.
3. **The 9x130 sample has high-acceptance optics (F8).** The 2026-07 jobs gave 9x130 `ip6_ep_130x10`, whose
   parameters equal `ip6_hiacc_100x10`; 9x100 got `ip6_hidiv_100x10`. Against eicBeamShape's high-divergence
   9x130 (a copy of its 10x100), abconv's beam angles are 0.82 as wide, vertex x is 12 % wider and vertex y
   33 % wider (`9x130_comparison.png`). This is the ratio of the two parameter sets (180/220, 118/145,
   86/105 µrad), not a code difference: the matched run (`9x130-hiacc`) agrees.
4. **The 2021 low-η difference is gone.** The 2021 README attributed it to head-on and beam-effects files
   generated separately. Here both come from one steering file and one seed, and the η ratio is flat in
   every setup.
5. **F7 reproduced on fresh inputs.** abconv run on two head-on files from different Pythia8 seeds gives the
   same interaction point and the same beam four-vectors (to 1e-9 relative) in 2000 of 2000 events, in all three
   setups. Pythia8 with eicBeamShape, seeds s and s+1, shares 0 of 2000. abconv's beam effects depend only on
   the event's position in the file.
6. Small upstream nits found on the way:
   - abconv 0.2.1 prints `--version` 0.1.3.
   - Its histogrammer fills `vtxX` with 0.1 mm bins (σ ≈ 0.13 mm) and leaves the 2D vertex histograms empty
     (their fills are commented out).
   - It selects the beams by order (main selects by PDG id).
   - No hiAcc steering file exists for 9x130. The `9x130-hiacc` setup uses the hiAcc 10x100 file at 9x130
     energies, the copy eicBeamShape itself makes.
